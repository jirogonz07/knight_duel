How to play online (read this first)
  1. Both players open this same link on their own computer (Chrome or Edge works best).
  2. Player 1 = host: press Host a match, wait a few seconds for a long code to appear, press Copy, and send it to Player 2 (Messenger, Discord, text).
  3. Player 2 = guest: press Join a match, paste the host's code in the first box, press Create reply code, then Copy it and send it back to the host.
  4. Host: paste the reply code in the second box and press Connect. The fight starts by itself on both screens.

How fighting works
  • Every hit drains the yellow stamina bar, and each hit in a row drains more. Attacking, dodging and blocking cost your own stamina too.
  • At zero stamina you are stunned for 1 second and cannot act. When you stun someone, get close and press F to start a combo cutscene (big damage). Miss that 1 second and he gets up with a little stamina and brief resistance.
  • Block (L or Shift) reduces damage. Tap block just before a hit to parry, which drains the attacker's stamina. Dodge with Space, feint with U.
  • Controls: WASD move · left click light · right click heavy.
